Billionaires don't pay tax like you do. And it's completely legal.
It's called: Buy. Borrow. Die.
Step one. Buy. They buy assets, like stocks, that grow in value. No sale means no tax.
Step two. Borrow. Instead of selling, they borrow against those stocks. A loan isn't income, so there's no income tax.
They live on the loan, while their stocks grow faster than the interest.
Step three. Die. In America, when they pass away, their heirs inherit the stocks at today's value. All that growth? Never taxed.
ProPublica found the twenty-five richest Americans paid a true tax rate of just three point four percent.
Meanwhile, your tax is taken before your paycheck even arrives.
So the real question isn't how much you earn. It's what you own.
