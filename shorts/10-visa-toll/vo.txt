Visa has never lent you a single cent. Yet it keeps about half of every dollar it makes.

When you pay with a Visa credit card, the money you borrow comes from your bank. Not from Visa.
Visa doesn't issue your card, and it doesn't set your interest rate.
It just runs the road your payment travels on, and charges a tiny toll every single time.
In its latest year, those tiny tolls added up to forty-five point nine billion dollars.
And twenty-three point five billion of that was profit.
No loans. No bad debts. The richest businesses don't carry the risk. They own the road.
