Warren Buffett bet a million dollars that a boring fund would beat Wall Street's best.
His pick: a simple S and P five hundred index fund. Then do nothing for ten years.
The other side picked five funds that owned over two hundred hedge funds.
Ten years later, the index fund was up one hundred twenty-five point eight percent.
The best pros? Eighty-seven point seven. One fund made just two point eight.
The reason? Fees. Paid every single year, win or lose.
Buffett's charity got over two point two million dollars.
Performance comes and goes. Fees never do.
