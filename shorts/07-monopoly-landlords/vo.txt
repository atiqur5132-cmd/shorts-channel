Monopoly was invented to warn you about landlords. Not to make you one.
In nineteen oh four, a woman named Lizzie Magie patented The Landlord's Game.
Her point was simple. Owners collect rent, while everyone else slowly goes broke.
People loved it. They copied it and passed it around for decades.
Then a man named Charles Darrow sold a version as his own invention.
Parker Brothers made it a hit. And Lizzie? They bought her patent for just five hundred dollars.
A game about greed became a lesson in greed.
So next time you play, remember who the game was warning you about.
