There's a one-line math trick that tells you when your money doubles.
It's called the Rule of seventy-two.
Take seventy-two. Divide it by your yearly return.
Earn eight percent a year, and your money doubles in about nine years.
Leave it in a savings account at one percent? Around seventy years.
Now flip it around. Credit card debt at twenty-four percent doubles in just three years.
And three percent inflation cuts your cash's buying power in half in about twenty-four years.
So for every rate in your life, ask one thing: is it doubling for me, or against me?
